The Endless Climb

Playable

Survival

The Endless Climb is a 2D rope-climbing game that forces you to jump from one rope to another to the top, while avoiding obstacles that will hinder you. And you, as the all-knowing player that has been blessed by the God of thunder himself, your phone got stolen by some monkey whilst you were on a climb, now you have to reach the top and get it back!

Cara Main

  • Lompat Gunakan tombol spasi untuk melompat antar tali.
  • Hindari Rintangan Perhatikan pola rintangan dan hindari benturan agar tidak jatuh.
